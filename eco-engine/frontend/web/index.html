FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Ask the in-app advisor: it explains every number on your plan.

What do I need to add my home?

Your EPC score (printed on the certificate) and last year's gas and electricity use in kWh from your bills. Everything else has a sensible default you can change later.

Is the result a valuation or mortgage advice?

No. Scores, savings and value ranges are indicative estimates from documented models. They are not a formal valuation or regulated financial advice.

How is the Green Home Score calculated?

Seven weighted parts (EPC rating, energy intensity, insulation, heating, glazing, renewables and smart controls) combine into a score out of 100. The app shows how many points each part contributes.

Why does the plan add measures I didn't pick?

Some upgrades depend on others. A heat pump in an uninsulated, draughty home runs inefficiently, so the planner adds insulation and draught-proofing first and tells you why.

Can the AI make up numbers?

No. It only rewords results the engines calculated. A validator checks every number in its reply; if any figure can't be matched, a template answer is shown instead.

Are my photos kept?

No. Photos are processed in memory for detection and discarded straight away. Nothing is saved unless you confirm a suggestion, and even then only the chosen upgrade is stored.

Which countries does Eco cover?

Eco is built for homes in the United Kingdom today: it uses UK Energy Performance Certificates, UK carbon factors and prices in pounds. Support for homes in India is coming soon, using BEE star ratings, India's grid carbon factor and prices in rupees. Until then, results for Indian homes are not accurate, so the app only accepts UK homes.

Who can see my homes?

Only you. Every request is checked against your account, and another user asking for your home gets "not found". You can delete any home, or your account, at any time.
